# Accuracy budget: findings (item 1 of the paper plan)

Numbers from `accuracy_budget.md` (generated by `accuracy_budget.py`; B3LYP pipeline results, default
Marcus-Levich-Jortner parameters unless stated). Figure: `paper/figures/mlj_gap_sensitivity.pdf`.

1. **k_ISC needs S1-T gaps to about 0.06-0.08 eV.** A 0.1 eV error in the S1-T gaps changes k_ISC by a
   factor of 1.7-2.7 for all nine dyes, because the dominant channel is S1→T1 on the downhill side of the MLJ
   curve. Keeping k_ISC within a factor of 2 requires gap errors below 0.062-0.084 eV; within a factor of 10,
   below 0.18-0.38 eV. The factor-2 tolerance depends on the assumed solvent reorganization energy:
   0.03-0.07 eV for λ_s = 0.05 eV and 0.08-0.12 eV for λ_s = 0.20 eV.
2. **This is chemical accuracy per state, so phase-estimation precision is not the bottleneck.** With
   independent errors on the two states, a 0.08 eV gap tolerance corresponds to about 2 mHa per state, slightly
   looser than the 1.6 mHa used in our CAS(12,11) resource estimate (about 3e7 Toffolis per run when scaled as
   1/ε). The bottleneck is the Hamiltonian model: CASCI(12,11) misses S1 by 1.4-2.0 eV (20-25 times the
   tolerance) and SC-NEVPT2 by up to 0.27 eV (about 3 times).
3. **The triplet-yield class (Φ_T above or below 0.5) is much more forgiving.** It does not flip for any gap
   error up to 0.5 eV in seven of the nine dyes, and needs about 0.4 eV in the other two. A screening that only
   asks "does this dye populate the triplet efficiently?" can tolerate errors of a few tenths of an eV, as long
   as no upper triplet is close to resonance. The distyryl diiodo dye is the counterexample in practice:
   the uncorrected TDDFT places T2 0.08 eV above S1, which makes ISC efficient, and the ΔSCF correction moves
   it 0.5 eV above S1, which switches ISC off (see the paper).
4. **Absorption-window membership is the tightest requirement for the dyes that matter.** The dyes that absorb
   near the therapeutic window sit within 0.00-0.07 eV of its 650 nm edge in experiment (aza-BODIPY-Ph4 at
   0.00 eV, the three distyryl dyes at 0.02-0.07 eV, all just outside). The B3LYP pipeline places the distyryl
   dyes 0.15-0.21 eV inside the window, so a sharp window classifies all three wrongly; PBE0 (errors of
   -0.14 to -0.15 eV) does too. The sharp-window observable of arXiv:2512.15889 inherits this edge
   sensitivity, so a smooth, error-aware window score is better suited to screening than a threshold.
5. **Functional choice fixes absorption for compact dyes but not ISC for extended ones.** PBE0 brings the six
   compact dyes to 0.00-+0.05 eV and k_ISC of I2-BDP and I3-BDP to 1.6e9 s⁻¹ (3.5e9 and 3.9e9 measured), but
   the distyryl diiodo dye stays at 7.6e6 s⁻¹ (4.5e8 measured), because its T2 remains 0.44 eV above S1.

Caveats: the budget is model-dependent (one effective MLJ mode, a uniform shift of the triplets, and fixed
SOC and k_r); it treats energy errors as rigid shifts; and it uses only nine dyes.
