\section{Multimode line shape for BDP, I2-BDP and aza-BDP-Br$_2$}
\label{sec:si-fcwd}

The B3LYP/def2-SVP S$_0$ Hessian is diagonalized after projecting out translations and rotations. The $\Delta$SCF S$_1$ and T$_1$ minima are aligned on the S$_0$ minimum (mass-weighted Kabsch) and their displacements projected on the S$_0$ normal modes give Huang--Rhys factors $S_j=\omega_j\Delta Q_j^2/2$ (displaced harmonic oscillators, no Duschinsky rotation, S$_0$ frequencies for all states). Modes below 200~cm$^{-1}$ are treated classically and added to an optional outer-sphere $\lambda_\mathrm{out}$; the density of states is the Kubo generating-function line shape at 298~K with a minimum Gaussian width of 0.01~eV. The S$_1\leftrightarrow$T$_1$ Huang--Rhys factors use the difference of the two displacements. The gap and coupling are the pipeline values; MLJ is the single-mode model of the main text ($\lambda_s=0.10$~eV, $\hbar\omega=0.17$~eV, $S=0.8$). For both dyes the harmonic S$_1\leftrightarrow$T$_1$ reorganization energy is larger than the one from energy differences at the optimized minima (0.09 and 0.12~eV, main text), which points to anharmonicity or Duschinsky mixing along the displacement. The single-mode MLJ model of the main text assumes 0.24~eV, above both estimates; its factor-2 agreement for I2-BDP survives because the multimode density of states at the pipeline gap is within a factor of 1.7 of the MLJ one. The vertical-gradient model, which describes S$_1$ by TDA rather than $\Delta$SCF, gives an S$_1\leftrightarrow$T$_1$ reorganization energy of 0.18~eV for BDP (0.13~eV from the minima) and 0.14~eV for I2-BDP (0.19~eV), and its density of states at the pipeline gap is 2.2--2.7 times larger for BDP and about 3 times smaller for I2-BDP; a line shape built from vertical gradients, as for the upper triplets, is therefore uncertain by about a factor of 3 in either direction.

\paragraph{Near-resonant S$_1\to$T$_2$ channel of aza-BDP-Br$_2$.} No $\Delta$SCF minimum exists for T$_2$, so its relaxation follows from the vertical-gradient model on the B3LYP S$_0$ Hessian of aza-BDP-Br$_2$. The TDA relaxation energies from the S$_0$ minimum are 0.153 (S$_1$), 0.086 (T$_1$) and 0.191~eV (T$_2$); the $\Delta$SCF optimization relaxes S$_1$ by 0.076~eV only, and projecting the $\Delta$SCF S$_1$ minimum on the S$_0$ modes gives 0.40~eV, so the harmonic model fails along the 190--260~cm$^{-1}$ modes of this dye. The adiabatic T$_2-$S$_1$ gap is therefore known only to within the range of the rows below. S$_1$ and T$_2$ are displaced almost in parallel (cosine 0.97 between their gradients, mutual reorganization 0.014~eV), so the width of the line comes from the outer sphere. The TDA S$_2$ lies 0.13~eV above S$_1$ at this geometry, so the S$_1$ gradient may carry S$_1$/S$_2$ mixing; we did not check the state character.
