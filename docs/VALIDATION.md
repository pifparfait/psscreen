# Validation report: classical and quantum backends vs experiment

Generated by `validation/report.py` from the JSON files in `validation/runs/`.
Level of theory: B3LYP/def2-SVP, gas phase, GFN2-xTB geometries, vertical energies at the S0
geometry. No parameter was fitted to these four molecules, with one exception stated below
(`k_ic`).

## How to read the results honestly

- **Absorption** is a genuine test: ΔSCF + RPA energies were not tuned on these dyes. Errors are
  0.02-0.08 eV (all calculated values slightly red of experiment). With four molecules and one
  functional this shows the method works for the BODIPY family, not that it is general.
- **ISC** is a qualitative-to-semiquantitative test. The model correctly gives no ISC for BODIPY, BDP
  and aza-BODIPY (no heavy atom) and large ISC for the 2,6-diiodo compound, and the S1-T1 gaps agree
  with the measured ones (0.72 vs 0.77 and 0.61 vs 0.59 eV). The absolute I2-BDP rate is
  6.9×10⁹ s⁻¹ against 3.5×10⁹ s⁻¹ measured (factor 2). The Marcus-Levich-Jortner parameters
  were set a priori, not fitted, and the sensitivity table shows the rate moves between about
  2×10⁹ and 3×10¹⁰ s⁻¹ over reasonable choices, so agreement within one order of magnitude is
  what can be claimed.
- **Fluorescence yields are not an independent test.** The internal conversion rate `k_ic` was set
  to the non-radiative rate measured for BDP (1.5×10⁸ s⁻¹). The calculated radiative rates are about
  2× too large (2.9×10⁸ vs 1.2×10⁸ s⁻¹ for BDP), which is the usual overestimate of vertical
  oscillator strengths.
- The raw TDDFT (without the ΔSCF correction) puts S1 at 385, 401, 448 and 548 nm for the four dyes,
  i.e. 0.35-0.76 eV too high, and predicts spurious triplet yields (Φ_T 0.82, 0.47 and 0.34 for the
  core, BDP and aza-BODIPY, which do not populate the triplet). This is the main reason the
  framework replaces the TDDFT S1/T1 energies with ΔSCF ones.
- The aza-BODIPY reference comes from secondary literature tables with slightly conflicting
  values (650 vs 655 nm) and no triplet yield; treat that row with care.
- Singlet-window check: the aza-BODIPY was first run with a 25 eV singlet window (16,560 orbital pairs,
  to fit 15 GB) and again on a 251 GB server with the full 60 eV window (33,670 pairs). λ_max (678 nm)
  and S1 (1.829 eV) are unchanged to 10⁻⁹ eV, f(S1) moves from 0.647 to 0.641 and k_ISC from 1.5e5 to
  1.4e5 s⁻¹, so the smaller window is adequate for these properties. The tables above use the 60 eV run.

### Withdrawn: the three styryl-extended dyes (BDP-0, I-BDP-0, I2-BDP-0)

A first version of this section reported these three dyes, with a large error in k_ISC for the diiodo
one (Φ_T 0.18 vs 0.76 measured) and a +50 nm outlier for I-BDP-0. **Those results were wrong and have been
removed.** The SMILES strings had no E/Z labels on the styryl C=C bonds, and the 3D embedding produced
geometries with one or two **cis (Z) styryl groups** (ring-CH=CH-phenyl dihedrals of about 0°, instead of
180°) for all three dyes. The measured compounds are the trans isomers. The apparent failures of the method
for these dyes, and the conformer-sensitivity that was inferred from them, were artefacts of that error.

Two earlier statements in this repository were therefore unfounded and were withdrawn: that the method
underestimates ISC for the distyryl-diiodo dye, and that the higher-triplet shift hypothesis was a
candidate explanation (that test, `validation/analysis_higher_triplets.py`, still stands as a check on the
other dyes: shifting T2 and above like S1 breaks the BODIPY core, BDP and I2-BDP).

The SMILES now carry E labels, `tests/test_molecule_stereo.py` checks that the generated geometries are
trans, and the three dyes are being recalculated from a conformer search (`validation/conformers.py`).

## Findings about the quantum workflow of arXiv:2512.15889

1. **The algorithms work in simulation.** The QSVT threshold projection reproduces the exact weight of
   the smoothed window to within 10⁻⁴ (up to polynomial degree 2000), and the Trotterized
   `exp(-i H_SOC t)` proxy reproduces the exact `|<T|H_SOC|S>|` to 3-4 digits, on CAS(4,4) and CAS(6,6)
   (8 and 12 qubits; 6 and 10 after Z2 tapering).
2. **The window observable is only as good as the active-space energies.** With the paper's 700-850 nm
   window the projected weight is 0 for every molecule at CAS(4,4)/(6,6), because the CASCI S1 lies at
   260-340 nm. A paper-sized active space does not fix this by itself: CASCI(12e,11o) on the BODIPY core
   gives S1 = 3.97 eV against 2.46 eV measured (3.35-4.32 eV across the four molecules). Adding SC-NEVPT2
   brings it to 1.99-2.71 eV, with errors of 0.06-0.27 eV. Dynamic
   correlation is therefore not optional for this property, and an exact quantum solver on a CASCI
   Hamiltonian would not by itself beat the classical TDDFT + ΔSCF results above.
3. **The ISC proxy ranks the heavy-atom effect correctly but not the mechanism.** At CAS(4,4) the largest
   S1→Tn coupling is 0.004-1.4 cm⁻¹ for the iodine-free dyes and 28 cm⁻¹ for I2-BDP, so the ordering is right. But
   the proxy has no energy gap, so it cannot tell which triplet channel is open, and the
   sum of squared couplings over states (the Xanadu-style figure of merit) for I2-BDP is dominated by a
   single high-lying state (~1100 cm⁻¹ to T5), which is irrelevant for the S1 decay.
4. **Resource estimates** (PennyLane double factorization, CAS(12,11), 1.6 mHa): about 600 logical qubits and
   4×10⁷ Toffolis per QPE run, with λ ≈ 11 Ha, for the iodinated and aza-BODIPY geometries.
